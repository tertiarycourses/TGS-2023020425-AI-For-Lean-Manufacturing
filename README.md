## Course focus

This two-day course connects registered Lean Manufacturing outcomes with practical manufacturing AI. Learners identify waste, analyse flow and inventory, plan capacity and maintenance, and design a governed Lean-AI pilot where people retain decision authority.

## Course package

- Highly visual, concept-led trainer deck in editable PowerPoint and PDF
- Detailed Learner Guide in Word, Markdown and PDF
- Slide-aligned Lesson Plan in Word and PDF
- Ten self-contained activity folders with scenarios, mock data, worksheets, evidence checklists, Markdown and matching PDFs
- Native editable PowerPoint charts and linked browser-based Lean/Six Sigma tools

## Activities

| # | Activity | Main evidence |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Waste Walk and AI Opportunity Scan | Waste register, Pareto summary, problem statement |
| 2 | SIPOC and Value-Stream Decision Map | SIPOC, current-state map, improvement hypothesis |
| 3 | Capacity, Takt and Bottleneck Analysis | Capacity table, takt comparison, countermeasure |
| 4 | ABC Inventory Classification with Criticality | Ranked ABC analysis and differentiated policy |
| 5 | EBQ and Kanban Control Design | EBQ, Kanban rule and sensitivity check |
| 6 | Root Cause with 5 Whys and Fishbone | Root-cause logic, evidence plan and countermeasure test |
| 7 | AI Visual Quality Pilot | Data plan, confusion matrix and human-review controls |
| 8 | Predictive-maintenance Decision Design | Criticality, condition trend and maintenance decision |
| 9 | Grounded GenAI for Standard Work | Grounded prompt, source hierarchy and reviewer checklist |
| 10 | Lean-AI Pilot Charter and Control Plan | Charter, risk controls, ROI, PDCA and sustainment criteria |

Open [activities/README.md](activities/README.md) to start. Detailed procedures appear in the Learner Guide and activity packs; the slide deck remains visual and concept-led.
