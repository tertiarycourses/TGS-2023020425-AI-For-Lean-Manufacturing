## About the course

Learners identify waste, analyse flow and inventory, plan capacity and maintenance, and design a governed Lean-AI pilot where people retain decision authority. The skills framework is TSC *Lean Manufacturing* (PRE-OPR-4064-1.1). The trainer deck is visual and concept-led; detailed procedures are in the Learner Guide and the activity packs.

## Learning outcomes

1. Identify eight types of production wastes within the organisation, benefits of lean manufacturing and steps to achieve lean performance results.
2. Apply lean principles to compute production capacity and TAKT time.
3. Perform ABC analysis for inventory classification using selective inventory control.
4. Calculate economic batch quantity and number of kanbans required.
5. Identify objectives of production and inventory control and types of inventories by function and condition.
6. Implement lean manufacturing tools for improved inventory flow and material flow.
7. Implement lean manufacturing in an organisation at various levels and within various areas for improvement.
8. Determine the necessity for preventive maintenance within the organisation.
9. Identify performance measurement areas in lean manufacturing and steps required for successful lean implementation.

## Topics covered

1. **Lean Foundations, Waste and Flow** — the eight wastes, SIPOC and value-stream mapping, capacity, takt and bottlenecks.
2. **Inventory and Production Control** — ABC classification, economic batch quantity, Kanban and root-cause analysis.
3. **AI-enabled Lean Implementation and Maintenance** — AI visual quality, predictive maintenance, grounded GenAI for standard work and a Lean-AI pilot charter.
