# Approved Source Pack

## SRC-01 - Manufacturer manual excerpt

Inspect spindle vibration only after lockout/tagout and according to the manufacturer's approved inspection method. Do not infer torque values not present in the approved manual.

## SRC-02 - WI-MNT-014 excerpt

Escalate recurring spindle alarms to the maintenance engineer. Record alarm code, operating state, recent work order and inspection result. The authorised engineer approves release to service.

## Prohibited source

Internet forum content is not an approved basis for maintenance action.
